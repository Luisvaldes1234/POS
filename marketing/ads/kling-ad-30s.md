# Ad de 30 s de POS Mostrador para Kling

Video vertical 9:16 para Reels, TikTok y Stories de Argentina, con voseo. Kling arma las escenas con gente y mostrador. La pantalla de la app se graba de verdad y se pone en la edición: Kling deforma las interfaces y los textos.

## Cómo armarlo

1. Generar cada toma en Kling con su prompt (en inglés: Kling los sigue mejor). Modo **Image-to-Video** si tienes una foto de referencia del personaje; si no, **Text-to-Video**. Relación **9:16**, duración **5 s** (las tomas 3 y 4, **10 s**), modo **Professional / High quality**.
2. Usar el **mismo personaje en todas las tomas**: generar primero la toma 1, sacar un fotograma del kiosquero y usarlo como imagen de referencia en las demás (Image-to-Video o Elements / referencia de personaje).
3. Grabar la pantalla real del POS en tablet o celular: escanear, cobrar en efectivo, aparece el vuelto. Insertarla en las tomas 3 y 4 encima de la pantalla del dispositivo, o como corte directo.
4. Textos en pantalla, logo, música y voz en el editor (CapCut, Premiere). Kling no escribe bien.
5. Voz: la locución de abajo con voz argentina (ElevenLabs o grabada). Música: lo-fi o funk suave, 100-110 BPM.

## Prompts para Kling

Personaje fijo (pegarlo al principio de cada prompt): **"A friendly Argentine kiosk owner in his early 40s, short dark hair, light beard, wearing a dark green apron over a white t-shirt"**.

**Toma 1 · El problema (5 s)**
> A friendly Argentine kiosk owner in his early 40s, short dark hair, light beard, wearing a dark green apron over a white t-shirt, stands behind the counter of a small neighborhood kiosk in Buenos Aires, crowded shelves of candy, snacks and cigarettes behind him. He frowns while punching numbers into a small calculator and counting coins, three customers wait in line, one checks his watch. Medium shot, handheld camera, slight push-in. Warm fluorescent morning light, realistic, cinematic, 35mm film look, shallow depth of field.

**Toma 2 · El cuaderno y el estante (5 s)**
> Close-up of a worn spiral notebook on a wooden kiosk counter, handwritten names and amounts crossed out many times, a pen resting on it; the camera slowly tilts up to a shelf with several empty gaps between soda bottles and snacks. The same kiosk owner (short dark hair, light beard, dark green apron) sighs in the background, out of focus. Slow tilt, natural warm light, realistic, cinematic, 35mm.

**Toma 3 · Escanear y cobrar (10 s)**
> The same Argentine kiosk owner (early 40s, short dark hair, light beard, dark green apron over white t-shirt) smiles as he scans a soda bottle with a handheld barcode scanner, a red laser line on the barcode; a tablet on a stand on the counter faces him, its screen glowing plain white. A customer hands him a banknote, he glances at the tablet and gives change confidently. Over-the-shoulder shot, smooth slow dolly, bright clean light, realistic, cinematic, 35mm, shallow depth of field.

**Toma 4 · Todo bajo control (10 s)**
> A happy customer leaves the small kiosk with a bag, waving goodbye. The same kiosk owner (short dark hair, light beard, dark green apron) relaxes, leans on the counter and swipes through a tablet whose screen glows plain white, nodding with satisfaction; fully stocked shelves behind him. Medium shot, gentle orbit to the left, warm afternoon light through the window, realistic, cinematic, 35mm.

**Toma 5 · Cierre (5 s)**
> Golden hour on a quiet Buenos Aires neighborhood street. The same kiosk owner (short dark hair, light beard, now without apron) pulls down the metal shutter of his small kiosk and smiles at the camera, relaxed. Wide shot, slow push-in, warm sunset light, lens flare, realistic, cinematic, 35mm.

**Prompt negativo (en todas):**
> text, letters, words, subtitles, watermark, logo, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed face, blurry, low quality, cartoon, 3D render, oversaturated
